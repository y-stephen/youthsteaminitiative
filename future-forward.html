Future Forward

Project Description

Future Forward is a student-led speaker series that helps students and families explore careers and industries. Each month, a guest professional talks about their field, their career path, and what it takes to get there, followed by time for questions from the audience.

Seminars are held virtually on Zoom and are open to students, parents, and anyone curious about what comes after high school. We plan at least six monthly sessions and may add more.

Seminar Format

About 60 to 75 minutes

  • Introduction (about 5 min)
    A student host introduces the speaker and topic.
  • Guest Presentation (about 40 min)
    Industry overview, career journey, and advice for students.
  • Q&A (about 10 min)
    Attendees ask questions through the Zoom chat.

Speaker Name: Scott Stransky

Cyber Risk Industry

Date and Time

Saturday, October 24, 2026 at 8:00 PM ET, online via Zoom

About the Talk

Cyberattacks are one of the fastest-growing risks in the world today. Scott Stransky will explain how experts use data, machine learning, and simulation to measure and manage cyber risk, and share his career path and the skills that lead to success in this field.

About the Speaker

Scott Stransky is Managing Director and Head of the Marsh Cyber Risk Intelligence Center. He previously led the Cyber Modeling group at AIR Worldwide (Verisk), was named the 2023 Cyber Risk Industry Person of the Year (Actuary/Modeler), and holds degrees in mathematics and atmospheric science from MIT.
