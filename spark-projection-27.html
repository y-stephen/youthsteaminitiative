Spark of Creativity Projection Mapping

Project Description

Spark of Creativity is a student-led projection mapping project created by the Youth STEAM Initiative (YSI) in partnership with the Munroe Center for the Arts. As a featured installation at Munroe Arts Illumination Night on Saturday, January 9, 2027, a team of Lexington students will transform the historic Munroe building into a dynamic, colorful canvas of light, art, and animation.

Building on YSI’s previous community projection mapping projects, including Shine Together in January 2026, the show explores how a single spark of inspiration can grow into many different forms of artistic expression. By combining original digital animation, creative storytelling, and artwork shared by the Munroe Center for the Arts, the student team brings together technology and visual arts to create a unique community experience.
