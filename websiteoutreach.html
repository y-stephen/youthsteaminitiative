Website and Outreach Team

The Website and Outreach Team is an ongoing project. We are the team that maintains the website you are viewing right now. Since the Youth STEAM Initiative does many activities and projects, we constantly update the website with any new information. In addition, we fix problems with our website and make it look good. We have also recently created the Outreach Team to go with the website team. The Outreach Team is in charge is in charge of things like our Wechat group, our Facebook account, our Instagram account, our YouTube account, and promoting our team to let people know about us.
